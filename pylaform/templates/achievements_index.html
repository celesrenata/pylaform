Achievement Writing Tips

Effective Achievement Structure:

  • Use the CAR method: Challenge, Action, Result
  • Start descriptions with strong action verbs
  • Quantify results whenever possible (%, $, time saved)
  • Highlight achievements that align with target roles
  • Connect achievements to specific roles and organizations

What to Include:

  • Awards and recognitions (formal and informal)
  • Projects that exceeded expectations
  • Innovations or process improvements
  • Leadership examples with measurable outcomes
  • Use the AI Improvement tool to format achievements as impactful statements
