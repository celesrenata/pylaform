Employment History Tips

Formatting Guidelines:

  • List most recent positions first
  • Use MM/YYYY format for dates (e.g., 06/2019)
  • Use "Present" for current positions
  • Include company locations when possible
  • Use official job titles for consistency

Best Practices:

  • Focus on relevant positions for your target role
  • Add achievements for each position to highlight impact
  • Include measurable results whenever possible
  • For current positions, describe ongoing responsibilities
  • After saving, use the "Manage Achievements" button to add accomplishments
